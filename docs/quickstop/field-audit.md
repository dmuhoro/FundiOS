> Status: **FIELD CAPTURE (template) — to be filled from the Quickstop
> internship.** This is not marketing copy; it is the raw input dataset for
> FundiOS backlog and pricing. Answer with observed facts, not assumptions in
> the first pass. Fill as much as you can in one shift; partial truth beats
> polished fiction.
>
> Origin: locked-chat questions — *"Does Quickstop garage have AI & Cloud
> integrated into their systems? What operational inefficiencies do they face?
> What are their operational workflows like?"*

## How to use

- Fill sections during observation and short one-on-ones with the owner, the
  receptionist, and a mechanic. Ask, then **ask why**, then watch one real
  instance.
- Convert what you see into the "numbers" sections. If the owner doesn't know
  a number, write `unknown` — not a guess. That is itself a finding.
- Keep a running **Observations log** with dates and quotes.
- Practice `info consent`: you are there to understand; don't interrogate,
  don't promise results, don't leak competitor-sensitive notes.
